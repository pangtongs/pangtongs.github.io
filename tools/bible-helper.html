Biblical Support Finder

Find a Bible passage that can be read in support of any action

Runs a small AI model in your browser — nothing you type leaves your device except the verse lookup. The first search downloads the model (about 1 GB) and needs a browser with WebGPU.

Interpretations come from a small model and can be a stretch — read the passage in context.
Model: Qwen2.5 1.5B via WebLLM · Scripture: World English Bible via bible-api.com
