    Read this map correctly: scope, accuracy and area

    “Nusantara” is used here as a geographic organizing term, not as a claim that the region was one country or that all Philippine polities were Indonesian dependencies. Only selected polities are included.

    Historical core polygons are hand-drawn illustrations around documented regions. Their vertices are NOT taken from historical boundary surveys. They are not full imperial extents, minimum-area estimates, or statistical confidence intervals. No historical square-kilometer totals are calculated. Uncolored land does not mean uninhabited or politically unorganized.

    Dashed connections show selected relationships or textual claims. They are not reconstructed sailing routes. A place named in a court poem is not automatically an administered province.

    Modern and colonial polygons use generalized Natural Earth country geometry. Colonial outlines are geographic proxies, not reconstructions of local administration or military control. Present coastlines are used throughout. The 2026 endpoint is a reference view, not a live border service.

    The same equal-area projection and geographic frame are retained at every date. This supports visual comparison of mapped land, but does not turn uncertain historical regions into measured territorial sizes.
