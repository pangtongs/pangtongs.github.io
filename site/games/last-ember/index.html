HOW TO PLAY

MOVE AND SURVIVE

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or drag a thumb on the left of the screen. Your spells cast themselves. Collect gems for XP.

DASH

Space, Shift or the DASH button darts you through foes and attacks without taking damage. Red and yellow zones are attacks: dash out of them.

THE HEARTH

1. Tap PLANT (E) to leave a fire circle on the ground. 2. Stand inside for +30% damage, healing and faster shield recovery. 3. Tap NOVA (E) to explode the fire and move on. Its blast reaches full power after 12 seconds. Enemies can destroy the fire; you can leave its circle at any time.

BUILD YOUR MAGIC

Level ups offer spells, passives and bargains. Spells rank up to 5, then evolve. Rank two related spells to 4 to awaken a fusion. Your Path comes at level 5 and your Ultimate at level 12.

STAGES

Each mini boss slain brings the final boss 20 seconds closer. Boss chests grant artifacts, then you choose a road to the next stage. From stage 3, staying in one small area for 8 seconds draws a marked strike. Move or dash out before it lands. Repeated strikes grow stronger until you move to a new area.

BETWEEN RUNS

Souls buy perks and new characters in the Den of Magic. Raise Heat for more souls, and play the Daily Ember: one seeded run per day.
